Privacy Policy

Hope & Joy Cleaning Services LLC — Orlando, Florida

Last updated: April 2026

At Hope & Joy Cleaning Services LLC, we are committed to protecting your personal information and being transparent about how we use it. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights as a user.

1. Who We Are

Hope & Joy Cleaning Services LLC is a professional cleaning company based in Orlando, Florida. We provide residential and commercial cleaning services in Orlando, Kissimmee, Davenport, Haines City, and surrounding areas.

For any privacy-related inquiries, you may contact us via WhatsApp at (321) 710-6128.

2. Information We Collect

When you submit a quote request through our website, we collect the following personal information:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Type of service requested
  • Cleaning frequency preference
  • Any additional information you choose to provide in the message field

We do not collect payment information, Social Security numbers, or any sensitive personal data through this website.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information you provide is used exclusively to:

  • Respond to your quote request
  • Contact you to schedule or confirm cleaning services
  • Communicate service updates or changes

We do not use your information for marketing purposes without your explicit consent, and we do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties.

4. How We Store Your Information

Your information is transmitted securely and stored only for the time necessary to fulfill your service request. We take reasonable measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse in compliance with the Florida Information Protection Act (FIPA).

5. Third-Party Services

Our website may include links to third-party platforms such as Instagram and WhatsApp. These platforms have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external services.

6. Your Rights

Under applicable Florida and U.S. law, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Opt out of any future communications

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us via WhatsApp at (321) 710-6128.

7. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted information through our website, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
